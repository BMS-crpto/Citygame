# City Life Online
Multiplayer 3D Lagos-style life sim. Run `node server.js`, open http://localhost:3000, share a room code.

Files: `server.js` (game server, no dependencies) · `public/index.html` (client, Three.js)

## What's in v0.3
- Needs: energy, hunger, hygiene, fun (hit 0 hunger/energy and you collapse into hospital)
- 12 Lagos places: Amala Shitta, Quilox, GTBank, Marina Towers, Balogun Market, Computer Village,
  Hospital, Gym, Church, Mushin Hostel, Ikeja Office Park, Oshodi Motor Park
- 8 jobs with shift pay and experience gates, naira economy, bank with interest
- Shops with perks (phone, laptop, clothes, Jordans, Rolex, generator)
- NEPA outages (lights, offices, showers go down; generator beats it), rain, weekdays
- Phone: stats, bag, people list with send-money
- Minimap, wanted thieves, owned houses (sleep/shower/TV/cook at home)
- Reflective PBR cars: sedan, SUV, coupe, danfo, keke; spinning wheels, steering, body roll, brake lights, headlights

Controls (desktop): WASD move, Q/E camera, E use, F action, P phone.
