# CITY LIFE - Lagos Life Game

Real Lagos. In your browser. No download.

## What You Do
- Choose Nepo Baby / LAPO Baby / UNILAG Fresher
- Work 33 careers, pay rent every Saturday
- Manage Hunger, Energy, Hygiene, Bladder, Fun, Social
- Explore 140 places: CcHub, Quilox, Balogun, UNILAG, Eko Hotels

## Features
- Modern Chat + NPCs going about
- Buy in the world: shop, market, cars
- Bigger houses: sleep & sit animations
- Babe & Babies: date, marry, family
- Police Chase + LASTMA
- Gangster Hours & Area Boys
